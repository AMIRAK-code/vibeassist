# VibeAssist — design material and asset production brief

Status: proposed art direction. Product facts and current site have not been inspected. The generated concept board is a visual reference, not a production logo or exact website specification.

## Identity

Personality: expressive, capable, approachable, precise.
Central idea: creative momentum, expressed through bold type and folded V geometry.
Recommended logo: asymmetric geometric V plus custom-spaced VibeAssist wordmark.
Palette and type: see MASTER-PROMPT.md and tokens.css.

## Logo generation prompt

Design an original identity exploration for VibeAssist, a brand using an Expressive Studio aesthetic. Create a compact abstract V using two broad diagonal strokes with one subtle angular fold or cut. Bold silhouette, confident geometry, slightly asymmetric but optically balanced, no fine details. Start with black on a white background. Present three genuinely different constructions at equal scale, each beside a carefully spaced VibeAssist grotesk wordmark and a small icon-size test. Flat vector-like design, no texture, mockups, gradients, shadows, robots, sparkles, chat bubbles, or imitation of existing brands. Prioritize recognizability at 24px and a simplified 16px favicon. This is concept exploration for later manual vector refinement.

## Hero artwork generation prompt

Create a flat editorial brand illustration using broad folded bands and intersecting paper planes inspired by a geometric V. Strong asymmetric composition, generous negative space, crisp clean edges. Palette restricted to cobalt #2448FF, acid #DFFF00, warm paper #F5F1E8, and ink #171717. A subtle sense of movement from an open scattered arrangement toward a coherent form. No text, letters, logos, gradients, shadows, 3D rendering, stock photography, sparkles, or interface mockups. Create a transparent-background decorative asset suitable for placing beside live HTML text. Keep the silhouette legible on mobile. This is an optional brand accent, not a substitute for a real product demonstration.

## Social image composition

1200×630. Warm-paper field; small wordmark at upper left; one large, verified product promise at left; large cobalt V-derived geometry at right; small acid accent. Keep important content at least 64px from edges. Use live vector/text layout for precise lettering. Export to PNG and check scaled to 600×315.

## Component states to design

Primary button: white on cobalt; darker cobalt hover; clearly visible focus; disabled state with explicit semantics. Secondary action: ink outline on paper. Text links: visible affordance and focus. Include navigation open/closed, active tabs, form idle/loading/success/error, FAQ expanded/collapsed, and any real product empty/error states surfaced in demos. Do not rely on animation or color alone.

## Content required from the project

Real product name and purpose; primary audience; main user action; three supported benefits; representative product workflow; authentic screenshots or sample data; existing pricing and policies; verified testimonials if any; valid footer destinations. Derive these from project sources before asking the owner to fill gaps.

## Final review checklist

- A visitor can identify the product and next action within the first screen.
- The logo works at small sizes and in one color.
- Product imagery reflects actual functionality.
- The page has one clear visual focal point per section.
- Large headings wrap cleanly on narrow screens.
- Navigation, demo, CTA, and forms work on keyboard and touch.
- Contrast, focus, reduced motion, and zoom are checked.
- No fictional proof, filler copy, dead controls, or unlicensed assets.
- Final assets are exported individually; the concept board is not reused as the website.
